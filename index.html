A Signal-Flow-Structured State-Space Model of Optical Dynamic Range Compressors

Riccardo Simionato · Univ. Bordeaux, CNRS, Bordeaux INP, LaBRI

Abstract

Optical dynamic range compressors exhibit complex, time-varying ballistics that make them difficult to model with fixed analytical expressions. This work presents a state-space neural network that mirrors the compressor’s own signal flow to enhance both accuracy and interpretability. We evaluate the proposed model on the Teletronix LA-2A and the Tube-Tech CL 1B optical compressors, comparing it against black-box and gray-box baselines. Results show that this domain-informed structure substantially reduces capacity requirements while processing sample-by-sample and remaining sample-rate independent and interpretable. The model ranks competitively on every metric on both devices, matching or exceeding black-box baselines that use two to three orders of magnitude more parameters and operations per sample.

Index Terms: dynamic range compression, gray-box modeling, state-space models, LA-2A, CL 1B, differentiable digital signal processing
